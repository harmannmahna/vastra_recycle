VastraChakra

UI / UX Design Brief



1. Brand Essence

VastraChakra ('Vastra' = fabric, 'Chakra' = cycle) stands for circularity — every garment gets a second life through reuse, recycling, or respinning into new material. The UI should feel like that cycle: organic, flowing, and alive — never sterile or purely corporate-green.



  EARTHY       WARM       MODERN       OPTIMISTIC  

Brand personality

Warm & human — this is about people's clothes and stories, not just logistics

Grounded & trustworthy — sustainability claims need to feel credible, not preachy

Energetic & modern — a Gen-Z/young-adult audience (per our survey, 50% aged 18–21) expects motion, not a static NGO-style site

Circular by design — visual motifs should literally loop (circles, rotation, orbit paths) to reinforce the 'chakra' idea

2. Colour Palette

A light, warm base keeps the site feeling clean and modern (not 'eco-guilt-trip green'), with a rich forest green anchor and two energetic accent colours for CTAs and highlights.





Usage rule: 60% Cream/Sand, 30% Forest Green, 10% Terracotta/Gold accents. Never place Forest Green text on Teal — keep one dominant colour per section for the 3D scenes to read cleanly against.



3. Typography

Headings — Poppins (SemiBold / Bold)

Rounded, geometric, friendly — pairs well with soft 3D shapes. Use for H1–H3, stat numbers, and button labels.

Body — Inter (Regular / Medium)

Highly legible at small sizes, neutral enough to let the colour palette and 3D visuals lead. Use for paragraphs, form labels, captions.

5. Imagery & Iconography

Photography style

Warm, natural lighting — real people with real clothes, not stock-photo perfection

Show the 'journey' visually: a worn garment → pickup → sorting → new life (thrift / repaired / respun yarn)

Avoid clinical/industrial recycling-plant imagery — keep it human and community-led

Iconography

Rounded, single-line icons (line weight 2px), Forest Green or Teal on Cream/Sand backgrounds

Core icon set: pickup van, folded shirt, recycle loop, needle & thread (repair), storefront (thrift), hand-holding-leaf (impact)

3D & motion assets

Low-poly / soft-clay style 3D garments (t-shirts, sarees, jeans) — approachable, not hyper-realistic

Motion should always loop or ease — nothing linear/robotic, to reinforce the circular brand idea

Scroll-triggered animations over autoplay loops — keeps performance light and respects user control
